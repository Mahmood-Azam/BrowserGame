Pick up & play.

Fairway Flick

Two players share the device. Drag backward anywhere and release to launch. Each player takes three shots in a row, then turns switch automatically. Or use ← → to aim, ↑ ↓ for power, and Space to shoot. Choose your round count, difficulty, and scoring before the match.

Tank Riot

Coral: WASD to move, F or Space to fire.
Azure: Arrow keys to move, L or Enter to fire.
Turrets aim at the rival automatically. Drive over mystery crates to receive one of 36 powers. Timed boosts stack; weapon pickups replace your current gun. Play a friend online across two devices, share a keyboard, or battle a bot. For online play, choose the two-devices option, create a room on one device, then enter its six-character code on the other. Either player can pause; both must be ready for a rematch. Online rooms need internet and use PeerJS Cloud for the connection handshake.

On a phone?

Golf uses touch dragging. Tanks have movement sticks and fire buttons; tap “Touch” inside the arena to show them. Turn your phone sideways for a bigger battlefield. Escape pauses either game. Sound starts off; switch it on inside a game.
